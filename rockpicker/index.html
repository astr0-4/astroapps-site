Field procedure

Three steps. Gloves on.

01

Join the farm

Enter your farm's code and your name once. Every launch after that goes straight to the map — the whole crew uses the same code.

02

Tap to log

Spot a rock from the cab? Tap it on the satellite map, confirm, done. Placement by eye on the imagery beats bouncing in-cab GPS.

03

Pick & mark

The picker crew drives pin to pin, marks each rock picked (with undo for stray taps), and the pin drops off the map.

No bars, no problem

Dead zones don't stop the crew.

Every pin is saved on the device first and synced to the cloud whenever a connection returns. Satellite tiles for your fields download ahead of time — automatically on WiFi — so the map stays crisp at deep zoom in airplane mode.

Acceptance test: airplane mode on, app restarted, everything still works.
